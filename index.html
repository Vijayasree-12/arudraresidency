Our Story & Hospitality

A welcoming stay in the heart of Tiruvannamalai

Arudra Residency welcomes travellers and pilgrims to Tiruvannamalai with comfortable accommodation in a convenient location near the renowned Arunachaleswarar Temple.

Whether you are visiting for temple darshan, a spiritual journey, a family trip or a peaceful break, our aim is to offer a welcoming place to rest and make your visit comfortable.
